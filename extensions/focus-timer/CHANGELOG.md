# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-07)

Package `focus-timer-0.1.0.formpkg`, 11100 bytes, SHA-256 `d1e72e664e6bde8b2fe4e7b5c92278d4a6ec7517fc1c532d8fb462c58fbd3804`.

- Focus Timer tile for FormTray's Widgets board: focus sessions with short and long breaks (25, 5 and 15 minutes, a long break after every 4 focus sessions, all adjustable).
- Start, Pause and Resume from the ring or the button, Reset, Skip; an optional note for what you are working on.
- Today's focus minutes and sessions, a seven-day chart against a daily goal, and the sessions of any of those days.
- An optional chime and a FormTray toast when a session ends; an option to start the next session on its own.
- Sessions survive a reload of the tile or of FormTray; one that ended while the tile was closed is logged at its real end.
- Small, medium and large tile layouts; FormTray's light and dark themes.
- Commands: Start or Pause, Skip to Next Session, Show Today's Sessions. No permissions.
