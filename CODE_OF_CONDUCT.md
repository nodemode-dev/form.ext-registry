# Code of conduct

form.ext-registry is where people submit extensions for the Form apps and where the maintainers review them in the
open. Most people here are sharing something they made, often for the first time, and asking strangers to judge it.
This page says how we treat each other while doing that. It applies to this repository, its issues, pull requests
and review threads, and to anyone speaking for the registry elsewhere.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, with first-time submitters above all.
- Review the extension, never the person: point to the rule, the file and the line, and say what would pass.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise. A failed check is a fix to make, not an accusation.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it kindly and specifically.
- Credit the work of others: the extensions, starters and examples you build on, and the code and media you carry.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, on any grounds.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption of any kind.
- Submitting an extension meant to harm the people who install it, impersonating another publisher, or trying to
  get past the checks or the review by hiding what an extension does.
- Pressuring the maintainers or other submitters over a review, or reopening a declined submission under another
  name.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code by opening an
[issue](https://github.com/nodemode-dev/form.ext-registry/issues), or by reaching the maintainers on GitHub: the
[nodemode-dev](https://github.com/nodemode-dev) organisation, [@nodemode-ai](https://github.com/nodemode-ai) and
[@gbarnai](https://github.com/gbarnai). A harmful extension is a security matter: report it privately as
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says, so it can be blocked before it is discussed.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and decide what is fair: a private word, a public note, removal of content, a
submission closed, an extension added to the block list, or a temporary or permanent ban from the registry's
spaces. People who report in good faith will not face retaliation.
