# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-07)

First release, for FormVis 2.1.0 (Form Extension Contract API 1).

- Process CMYK separations with Black and Ink limit, or two to four spot inks from sets or any colour, matched by overprint on the paper colour.
- Per-plate screen angle, lines per inch, dot shape (Round, Ellipse, Square, Diamond, Line, Cross) and Ink %; Smooth or Hard (1-bit) edges; Misregister.
- Overprint preview at Fit, 100% and 200% with pan, per-plate visibility and single-plate view; progress and Cancel for long work.
- Add as Layers: a Paper layer and one Multiply layer per plate, one undo step.
- Save Plates as PNG: one film per plate and the overprint.
- Headless command Add Halftone Plates as Layers for the Extensions menu, the palette and agents.
