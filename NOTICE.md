## Methods

The sun and moon positions use the published low-precision formulas for solar and lunar coordinates (the Astronomical Almanac's approximate solar coordinates and a truncated lunar theory as given in standard astronomy texts), written for this extension. No code or data from other projects is included.

The package carries no media, fonts or models.
